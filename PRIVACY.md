# Privacy policy

Socratic Tutor by Mumbai AI Lab (plugin name `mailab-socratic-tutor`)

Effective 21 October 2026

## What the plugin is

The plugin is a set of written instructions, in Markdown files, that change how Claude responds when you are studying. It contains no code, no scripts, no connectors and no links to any server. You can read all of it in this repository.

## Data the plugin collects

None. The plugin has no way to collect, store or transmit data. Mumbai AI Lab receives no data from your use of it: no messages, no usage figures tied to you, no account details.

## Data the plugin asks you for

The tutor may ask what class or year you are in, so that it can pitch its questions at the right level. You do not have to answer. It does not ask for your name, school, email address, grades or any other personal detail, and it is instructed not to.

## Retention

The plugin keeps no data. It is instructed not to keep notes about you between conversations.

## Your conversations

Your conversations with Claude are processed by Anthropic under the terms and privacy policy of the Claude product you are using, as they would be without the plugin. The plugin does not change how Anthropic handles your data. See [Anthropic's privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy).

## Third parties

The plugin sends no data to any third party.

## Age

The plugin is used inside Claude and follows the age requirements of the Claude product you are using.

## Changes

Any change to this policy is committed to this repository with a new effective date, and the history of the file keeps the earlier versions.

## Contact

Open an issue at https://github.com/Rayhaan1345/socratic-tutor/issues. Mumbai AI Lab maintains the plugin.
