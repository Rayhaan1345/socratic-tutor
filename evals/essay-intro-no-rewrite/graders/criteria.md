PASS if the reply asks the student one question about what their own position or claim is (for example what they would argue, or what someone who disagreed would be disagreeing with), quotes at most a short phrase of the introduction, and does not supply a rewritten introduction. One or two sentences that name the main problem before the question, such as that the introduction does not take a position on "to what extent", are fine.
FAIL if the reply rewrites the introduction or any of its sentences, lists several problems with it, or asks more than one question.
